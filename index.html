The Villa
A modern retreat, eight kilometres from Kandy city.
TenTwo sits quietly above Kandy, built around a single idea: bring the outside in. Full-height glass walls open every living space onto a large pool, blurring the line between architecture and landscape. Three en-suite bedrooms and a private pool make it suited to families and small groups seeking a still, design-led escape close to the city.

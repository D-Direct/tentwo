The Villa
A modern retreat, eight kilometres from Kandy city.
TenTwo is built around a single architectural idea: bring the outside in.
Full-height glass walls dissolve the boundary between living space and landscape. Every principal room opens onto a long private pool, so water, light and the surrounding hills of Kandy become part of the interior. Reflections shift through the day; the line between architecture and nature remains deliberately thin.
The plan is simple and deliberate. Three en-suite bedrooms (each with air-conditioning and ceiling fans) are arranged for both connection and privacy. A fully equipped kitchen, six-seater dining table and calm living area with smart TV form the social heart of the house, while a rooftop terrace extends the living space upward. Practical elements — high-speed Wi-Fi, washing machine, hairdryer, iron and mosquito repellents — are present but secondary to the experience of the space itself.
Located eight kilometres above Kandy, the villa offers proximity to the city's temples, gardens and viewpoints within a twenty-minute drive, yet remains removed enough to feel private and unhurried. A modern residence designed for those who value clarity of form, continuous light, and the quiet presence of the landscape.

TenTwo Private Luxury Villa Kandy Sri Lanka

TenTwo is a private luxury residence located eight kilometres above Kandy, Sri Lanka. Designed around the idea of bringing the outside in, the villa features full-height glass walls that open every living space onto a long private swimming pool, creating a continuous relationship between architecture and the surrounding hills.

The property offers three en-suite bedrooms with air-conditioning and ceiling fans, a fully equipped kitchen, six-seater dining area, calm living space with smart TV, rooftop terrace with outdoor seating, high-speed Wi-Fi, washing machine, and secure parking for two cars. It is suited to families and small groups seeking a design-led, private escape close to Kandy's temples, botanical gardens and viewpoints, yet removed enough to feel still and unhurried.

Guests can enquire directly through the website or book via major platforms once listed. TenTwo combines contemporary architecture, privacy, and practical comfort in one of Sri Lanka's most culturally rich regions.

# 1. Brand Foundation

## 1.1 Brand Name

**TEN TWO**

Use the visual brand treatment already established: **TEN** in white/ivory and **TWO** in the brand gold.

The written brand name is **TenTwo** when used in normal prose, URLs, metadata and structured data.

Do not write “Ten Two Villa” as the primary brand name.

## 1.2 Primary Positioning

**TenTwo is a contemporary private residence for exclusive stays near Kandy, Sri Lanka.**

It is designed primarily for **families and groups of friends** who want the entire property to themselves rather than a shared accommodation experience.

The property should feel:
- private
- contemporary
- architectural
- calm
- connected to nature
- refined rather than flashy

The website must never make TenTwo feel like a conventional hotel or a generic holiday rental.

## 1.3 Brand Signature

**Where Architecture Meets Nature**

This is the emotional brand line.

It should not be used as the only explanation of the property. The visitor must also immediately understand that TenTwo is accommodation that can be reserved.

## 1.4 Hero Category Line

**A Contemporary Private Residence for Exclusive Stays**

This line establishes the category without using “luxury villa” as the main brand identity.

## 1.5 Core Guest Promise

**Every reservation includes exclusive use of the entire residence.**

This is a major differentiator and should appear prominently on the website.

# 3. Brand Pillars

## 3.1 Architecture

Contemporary architecture is part of the experience, not merely decoration.

Key ideas:
- floor-to-ceiling glass
- indoor/outdoor connection
- clean lines
- natural light
- reflections
- water
- open views

## 3.2 Privacy

TenTwo is booked as an entire residence.

The visitor should understand:
- the residence is not shared with unrelated guests
- the pool is private
- the stay is designed around uninterrupted time together

## 3.3 Landscape

The surrounding landscape should be treated as part of the residence.

Key visual/story elements:
- hills around Kandy
- green paddy fields visible from the master-bedroom balcony
- tropical greenery
- water and reflections
- changing light
- open skies

Do not imply that the paddy field belongs to TenTwo. Say “overlooking” or “with views across” the paddy fields.

## 3.4 Connection

TenTwo is particularly suited to families and friends spending meaningful time together.

Examples:
- breakfast together
- poolside afternoons
- shared meals
- rooftop evenings
- quiet conversations
- discovering Kandy together

## 3.5 Slow Living

The copy should communicate a slower rhythm without becoming vague or overly poetic.

Use concrete moments:
- morning coffee on the balcony
- time beside the pool
- long meals
- sunset from the rooftop
- returning to a quiet residence after exploring Kandy

# 4. Brand Voice

TenTwo should sound:
- calm
- confident
- sophisticated
- warm
- understated
- contemporary
- editorial

Avoid:
- exaggerated luxury language
- aggressive sales language
- generic travel clichés
- excessive adjectives
- “best”, “ultimate”, “paradise”, “dream holiday”, “once-in-a-lifetime”
- “cheap”, “deal”, “budget”
- repeated use of “luxury” in every section

Luxury should be communicated through the details and restraint of the writing.

# 6. Location & Privacy Policy

Do **not** publish the exact street address.

Do not expose the exact house number or detailed directions on the public website.

Public positioning:

> TenTwo is located in the peaceful hills near Kandy, Sri Lanka, approximately 8 km from Kandy city centre.

The journey to Kandy city centre is approximately 20 minutes by car, depending on traffic and road conditions.

Recommended privacy wording:

> For the privacy and security of our guests, exact directions and the precise location are shared after a reservation is confirmed.

Never make the location sound secretive or suspicious. The purpose is guest privacy and security.

# 22. FAQ — Approved Content

## How far is TenTwo from Kandy city centre?

> TenTwo is approximately 8 km from Kandy city centre. The journey is typically around 20 minutes by car, depending on traffic and road conditions.

## Is TenTwo rented as an entire residence?

> Yes. TenTwo is offered exclusively as an entire private residence. The property is not shared with unrelated guests during your stay.

## Is the swimming pool private?

> Yes. The swimming pool is exclusively for guests staying at TenTwo.

## How many guests can stay at TenTwo?

> TenTwo has three en-suite bedrooms and is designed for families and groups of friends travelling together.

Maximum occupancy: **6 adults**.

## What facilities are available?

> TenTwo includes three air-conditioned en-suite bedrooms with fans, a private pool, fully equipped kitchen, six-seater dining area, living space with smart TV, rooftop terrace, high-speed Wi-Fi and secure on-site parking.

## Is there a kitchen?

> Yes. The fully equipped kitchen is available for guests to prepare their own meals. Cereal, coffee and tea are available for guests to prepare themselves.

## Is Wi-Fi available?

> Yes. High-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the residence.

## What are the check-in and check-out times?

> Standard check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Late check-out may be available when there is no subsequent booking and can be requested in advance.

**CONFIRMATION REQUIRED:** Verify these times before publication.

## Is the exact location shown on the website?

> We share the general location of TenTwo near Kandy, but the exact address and detailed directions are provided after a reservation is confirmed, for the privacy and security of our guests.

## How do I make a reservation?

> Submit a reservation request with your preferred dates and number of guests. We will confirm availability and the next steps by email.

## Is TenTwo suitable for families?

> Yes. TenTwo is designed with families and groups of friends in mind, with three private air-conditioned bedrooms, shared living spaces and the entire residence reserved exclusively for your stay.

## Can I book through a booking platform?

> TenTwo will also be available through selected booking platforms. Booking.com and Agoda links will be added here once the listings are live. Use only the official booking links shown on the TenTwo website.

# 33. Guest Services

## Breakfast

Breakfast is not provided as a hosted service in the initial phase.

Guests can prepare their own breakfast using the fully equipped kitchen. Cereal, coffee and tea are available for guests to prepare themselves.

Do not describe TenTwo as offering breakfast.

## Airport Transfers

Airport pickup and drop-off are available for an additional fee and must be booked in advance.

Approved wording:

> Airport transfers can be arranged in advance for an additional fee, with both pickup and drop-off available.

## Private Driver

Guests can also arrange a private driver if they wish to explore other parts of Sri Lanka.

Approved wording:

> A private driver can also be arranged for guests who would like to explore Kandy and travel further across Sri Lanka.

Do not imply that a driver is included in the accommodation rate.

## Future Dining Service

A professional chef/dining service may be introduced at a later stage, where guests provide ingredients and arrange for a chef to prepare a meal.

Do not publish this as a current service until it is operational.

## Future Rooftop BBQ

A BBQ grill is planned for the rooftop at a later stage.

Do not list the BBQ as an existing amenity until it has been installed and is available to guests.


# 34. Accommodation Configuration — Confirmed

TenTwo accommodates a maximum of **6 adults**.

Guest bedrooms:
- 3 bedrooms
- 1 king bed in the master bedroom
- 1 queen bed in the first-floor standard bedroom
- 1 queen bed in the ground-floor standard bedroom
- all three bedrooms have air conditioning, fans and attached bathrooms

The first-floor standard bedroom has a separate wardrobe area.

There is also a small room with an attached bathroom intended for a driver. This is support accommodation and should **not** be advertised or counted as a guest bedroom. Do not mention it in the public website unless the business later decides that it is relevant to a specific guest service.

OUR STORY

A quiet, private place within easy reach of Kandy

TenTwo was created as a quiet, private place to stay within easy reach of Kandy. Just 25 minutes from the city, it gives you space, greenery and privacy, with Kandy's temples, gardens and markets close by.

When you book TenTwo, the entire residence is yours. There are no other guests and no shared spaces. Enjoy the whole villa at your own pace.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

Where is TenTwo located?

About 25 minutes from Kandy city, in the hills of Sri Lanka's Central Province.

Is TenTwo self-catering?

Yes. The villa is fully self-catering. Meals can be ordered for an additional fee.

Do I get the whole villa?

Yes. The entire residence is always yours. You never share the villa with other guests.

Is the pool private?

Yes. The pool is fully walled and used only by your group.

Are there security cameras?

CCTV covers outdoor areas, including the pool. There are no cameras inside, and the pool camera can be switched off on request.

Can you arrange drivers or tours?

Yes, drivers and tours can be arranged on request.

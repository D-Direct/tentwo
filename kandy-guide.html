Things to Do in and Around Kandy

Temples, gardens, tea country and day trips, all within easy reach of TenTwo (25 minutes from Kandy).

CULTURE & HISTORY

Temples and royal heritage

Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic

Sri Lanka's most sacred Buddhist site, beside Kandy Lake and part of the UNESCO-listed sacred city. Go during the morning or evening ceremonies to hear the traditional drummers.

Tip: Cover shoulders and knees; shoes are removed at the entrance.

Kandy Lake

An easy, flat walk around the lake in the centre of town, built in the early 1800s under the last king of Kandy. The scalloped "cloud wall" along its edge is a favourite photo spot.

Bahirawakanda Buddha Statue

A giant white Buddha on a hilltop above the city, with wide views across Kandy and the surrounding hills. Best late afternoon.

NATURE & GARDENS

Gardens, forests and hills

Royal Botanical Gardens, Peradeniya

Sri Lanka's most famous gardens, with an orchid house, giant bamboo, and long avenues of palms. Allow 2–3 hours.

Udawattakele Forest Reserve

A peaceful forest right behind the Temple of the Tooth, once reserved for royalty. Good for birdwatching and short walks.

Hanthana Mountain Range

Hiking trails through tea land and misty ridges, with views over the hill country. Start early for clear weather.

LOCAL EXPERIENCES

Kandyan culture and local life

Kandyan Cultural Dance Show

Drummers, dancers and fire walking, held most evenings in town.

Tea Factory Visit

See how Ceylon tea is made and finish with a tasting.

Kandy Town & Markets

Spices, batik, wood carvings and silver jewellery.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about visiting Kandy

How many days do you need in Kandy?

Two to three days covers the main sights. Add extra days for Sigiriya, Dambulla or Nuwara Eliya day trips.

Is TenTwo a good base for exploring Kandy?

Yes. It is 25 minutes from Kandy city, and drivers and tours can be arranged on request.

What should I wear to the Temple of the Tooth?

Clothing covering shoulders and knees. Shoes are removed at the entrance.
